FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to what folks usually want to know before calling

Do you charge for estimates?

No — all project estimates are free. We'll come out, look at the site, and give you a written quote at no cost.

What parishes do you serve?

Our primary service area is Iberia, Lafayette, Acadia, Vermilion, and St. Martin parishes. We also handle projects in St. Landry, Evangeline, Jeff Davis, and St. Mary parishes — call to confirm we cover your location.

How quickly can you start a project?

It depends on the season and our current workload, but most projects can be scheduled within 1–3 weeks of an accepted quote. Emergency situations are handled as quickly as we can get equipment on site.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — we're fully licensed and insured for all the dirt work services we provide. Documentation is available on request.

Do I need a permit for my project?

It depends on the parish, the scope of work, and whether you're near drainage or in a regulated zone. Ponds, levees, and culverts often require permits. We can guide you on what's typically needed, but you'll need to check with your parish for the final word.

How is pricing determined?

Most jobs are priced based on scope, access, material volume, and equipment time. We give you a written estimate before any work starts so there are no surprises.

Do you handle small residential jobs or only commercial?

Both. We do everything from a single residential pad or driveway culvert to large commercial site prep. No job is too small.
